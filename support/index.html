SK8: Hell Yeah · Kick, ollie, flip, grind.

SK8: Hell Yeah is a tiny skateboarding game for iPhone and Android. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, we're one email away.

How do I play?

Stick (left thumb): hold to kick, let go to coast, pull back to brake.

Jump (right thumb): ollie. Land on a rail to grind.

Flip: in the air only. Tap once for a kickflip, twice for a 180, three times for a 360, four for a backflip.

Enemies: land on their heads or jump over them. Touch their bodies and you're smoked. Five lives per run.

Level 1, Street: ride from the thumbs-up hand to the rock-on hand.

Level 2, Downtown: ollie over 12 manhole covers in 30 seconds. Ollie off the top of the stairs; don't roll down them. You get 5 tries; use them all and you're back on the Street, and beating the Street again gives you 5 fresh tries.

Level 3, Hell: ride 1000 m out of hell. Land on a monster to blow it up, hold Duck under the flying one, and don't touch them.

Each level unlocks when you finish the one before it. Pick any unlocked level from the start screen.

Does it save my score or progress?

Yes, on your phone only: your best score on each level, which levels you've unlocked, and your sound setting. There are no accounts, so deleting the app or clearing its data resets everything. (On Android, your phone's Google backup may bring your progress back when you reinstall.)

There's no sound.

Tap the screen once to start the music, then check the Sound button on the start screen: it says Sound on when the sound is off. If it's still silent, turn up your phone's volume.

The game is sideways / won't turn.

SK8 rides with the phone held sideways, on purpose. If the picture doesn't turn when you rotate the phone, switch off rotation lock (iPhone: Control Center; Android: Quick Settings → Auto-rotate) and turn the phone again.

How do I contact you?

Email support@oatmealmorning.com. We're a two-person studio, so you'll get a real person, usually within a few days. Bugs, high-five stories and trick suggestions all welcome.
