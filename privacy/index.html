Privacy Policy

SK8: Hell Yeah · Last updated 24 November 2026

SK8: Hell Yeah is a tiny skateboarding game for iPhone and Android. This page explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information. It's a short page because there isn't much to say.

Nothing is saved about you

SK8 has no accounts and no server. The only things it remembers, stored on your own phone, are your best score on each level, which levels you've unlocked, how many Downtown tries you have left, and whether you turned the sound off. None of this is tied to you, and deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To know whether anyone plays, we count simple events: the app was opened, a game was started, ended or left part-way, a level was won, a new best score was set, the cutscene was played or skipped, a locked level was tapped, and the share, feedback or credits button was tapped. Each event carries only gameplay numbers (level, score, previous best, distance, seconds played, best trick), where a button was tapped, and the app version; nothing you type or anything personal is recorded. For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog receives the type of device and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. The app stores one random ID on your phone so we can tell that the same phone played again. It is not linked to your name, your account, your advertising ID or any other app, and clearing the app's data resets it. We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Feedback

The Feedback button opens a short Google Form in your browser. Filling it in is optional. If you choose to leave your email address there, we use it only to reply to you.

Sharing

The share buttons use your phone's own share sheet to pass along a short message with your score and a link to the game's page. We don't see who you share it with.

App stores

Apple and Google may send us anonymous crash reports and basic install statistics under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

This website

This website (sk8.oatmealmorning.com) counts page visits and taps on the store buttons the same anonymous way, without cookies or any ID. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads. We do not ask for your name, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone.

Children

SK8 is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
