Privacy Policy

SK8 · Last updated 21 October 2026

SK8 is a tiny skateboarding game. This page explains what it does and doesn't do with your information. It's a short page because there isn't much to say.

Nothing is saved about you

SK8 has no accounts and no server. The only things it remembers, stored on your own device, are your best score on each level, which levels you've unlocked, and whether you turned the sound off. None of this ever leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To know whether anyone plays, we count simple events: a game was started or ended, a level was won, the cutscene was played or skipped, a locked level was tapped, and the share, feedback or credits button was tapped. Each event carries only the level number and gameplay numbers (score, distance, seconds played, best trick); nothing you type or anything personal is recorded. For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog receives the type of device and browser and an approximate location derived from your IP address. SK8 keeps no identifier between visits, so each play looks like a new visitor. We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites. You can block this with any content blocker; the game plays the same without it.

Sharing

The share button uses your phone's own share sheet to pass along the game's web address. We don't see who you share it with.

Hosting

SK8 is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

App stores

If you install SK8 from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports and basic install statistics under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children

SK8 is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
